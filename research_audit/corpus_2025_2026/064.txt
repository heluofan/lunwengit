不 断 优 化 成 像 系 统 的 性 能，以 实 现 更 高 效 的 数 据 采 集
和 处 理。 在 算 法 层 面，基 于 数 据 驱 动 的 方 法 正 成 为 提
升 成 像 质 量、速 度 与 自 动 化 水 平 的 关 键 发 展 方 向。 深
度 学 习 技 术 凭 借 其 自 动 特 征 提 取、强 大 的 非 线 性 建 模
能 力 及 高 效 数 据 处 理 优 势，有 望 突 破 光 学 成 像 在 分 辨
率极限 、噪声抑制 、复杂环境中信号衰减以及海量数据

Abstract 
Significance 　 Nonlinear optical microscopy (NLOM ) has emerged as a revolutionary tool in life sciences and clinical research , 
offering unprecedented capabilities for high -resolution , three -dimensional , and deep -tissue imaging within living samples . Traditional 
linear fluorescence microscopy often relies on fluorescent probes that can induce phototoxicity . In contrast , NLOM techniques —such 
as two -photon excited fluorescence (2PEF ), second harmonic generation (SHG ), third harmonic generation (THG ), and coherent

Raman scattering (CRS ) —provide intrinsic contrast based on the inherent molecular properties of biological tissues . This label -free 
capability is crucial for long -term , high -speed dynamic imaging of cellular and subcellular processes in vivo  without altering their 
native states .
The complexity of biological systems , however , means that a single imaging modality often provides an incomplete picture . For 
instance , while SHG is excellent for visualizing highly ordered structures like collagen , it cannot reveal metabolic activity . By 
integrating multiple NLOM modalities into a single platform , multimodal nonlinear optical microscopy (MNLOM ) overcomes this 
limitation . MNLOM constructs a multi -dimensional profile by simultaneously capturing complementary information on tissue 
morphology , cellular function , and molecular composition . This enables multi -parameter and accurate identification of complex 
biological events , making it a powerful platform for investigating fundamental cellular mechanisms , advancing early diagnosis , and 
guiding precise , real -time diagnosis in clinical settings . Therefore , the development and application of advanced MNLOM systems are 
of paramount significance for both fundamental biomedical research and clinical diagnostics . This review aims to systematically 
summarize the key developments in MNLOM , providing a critical reference for researchers to harness its full potential .
Progress 　 Recent years have witnessed remarkable progress in both the technological development and biomedical application of 
MNLOM . Our review begins with a detailed exploration of system development , focusing on integration strategies and technological 
evolution . We discuss the transition from early -stage , lab -built systems to sophisticated , user -friendly platforms designed for complex 
biological experiments . Key innovations in system integration and technological evolution are highlighted , including the 
synchronization of multiple laser sources , the design of efficient signal collection pathways , and the development of compact and 
robust microscope heads suitable for clinical environments . We specifically elaborate on the engineering challenges and solutions for 
building systems oriented toward clinical and in vivo  imaging , such as the integration with endoscopes for intraoperative diagnosis . 
Following system development , we focus on advanced system optimization methods . Innovations in system architecture , such as the 
use of shared laser sources and optimized scanning modules , have significantly improved imaging speed and depth . A particularly 
transformative advancement is the integration of deep learning , which enables the use of artificial intelligence algorithms for real -time 
image acquisition .
The latter half of the review focuses on the diverse biomedical applications of MNLOM . We first explore how multimodal 
imaging enables specific biomarker characterization without external labels . For example , by combining 2PEF from endogenous 
fluorophores like nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NADH ) and flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD ), SHG from collagen , and 
coherent anti -Stokes Raman scattering (CARS ) or stimulated Raman scattering (SRS ) for lipid mapping , researchers can 
simultaneously visualize cellular morphology , extracellular matrix remodeling , and metabolic state . This capability has been 
powerfully applied across various fields . In oncology , MNLOM facilitates early diagnosis and precise pathological grading and staging 
by providing label -free histopathology of tumor margins . In cardiovascular disease research , it allows for the dynamic visualization of 
atherosclerotic plaque formation and vessel wall mechanics . For central nervous system studies , MNLOM enables deep -brain imaging 
of neuronal activity and myelin sheath degradation . Furthermore , we present case studies in ophthalmology for diagnosing retinal 
diseases and in pharmacology for assessing drug efficacy and toxicity , demonstrating the technology ’s broad utility for prognostic 
assessment and drug development .
Conclusions and Prospects 　 In conclusion , MNLOM has evolved from a specialized physical tool into an indispensable platform for 
modern biomedical research . By synergistically combining the strengths of various label -free imaging modalities , MNLOM provides a 
multi -parametric and comprehensive window into the intricate dynamics of life , from the subcellular to the tissue level . The 
integration of advanced optics , high -speed electronics , and intelligent algorithms has pushed the boundaries of high -resolution , real -
time imaging , enabling breakthroughs in our understanding of disease pathogenesis and paving the way for novel diagnostic and 
therapeutic strategies .
Looking forward , the field is poised for further innovation . Future prospects include the development of next -generation systems 
with even higher sensitivity , deeper penetration , and faster acquisition speeds . The fusion of MNLOM with other advanced 
techniques will unlock additional dimensions of information . A major thrust will be the continued clinical translation of MNLOM , 
requiring the development of more compact , cost -effective , and FDA -approved systems for routine adjuvant diagnosis in operating 
rooms and clinics . Furthermore , creating standardized , AI -driven analysis pipelines and large -scale databases is crucial for translating 
multimodal imaging data into clinically relevant diagnostic information . The maturation of these technologies will accelerate the 
translation of this technique from the laboratory to clinical applications such as intraoperative navigation and pathological diagnosis , 
ultimately providing a powerful tool for in-situ , real -time , and multi -dimensional optical detection in precision medicine .

基于伪影去除算法的步进误差与通量波动伪影校正
张尊 ，王志立 *
（合肥工业大学  物理学院 ，安徽  合肥  230009）
摘 要 ：Talbot -Lau X 射线成像技术凭借其同步获取透射 、微分相位及暗场信号的多模态成像能力 ，在生物医学成
像中展现出独特优势 。然而 ，相位步进误差和 X 射线源通量波动会在重建图像中产生莫尔伪影 ，降低了图像质量 。为了
解决这一问题 ，采用一种基于步进曲线相位与图像伪影相关性的伪影去除算法 ，并通过模拟实验验证其效果 。研究表
明，所提算法可以有效地消除由步进误差和通量波动导致的伪影 ，同时还能保留图像的真实信息 。此外 ，通过分析噪声
水平、拟合区域及莫尔条纹形态 ，进一步验证了该算法在不同实验条件下的稳定性 ，具有较高的实际应用价值 。
关 键 词 ：X 射线光学 ；Talbot –Lau 干涉仪 ；步进误差 ；通量波动 ；莫尔伪影
中图分类号 ：O434.1    文献标识码 ：A
Artifact correction of stepping errors and flux fluctuations 
based on removal algorithm
ZHANG Zun ,WANG Zhili *
(School of Physics , Hefei University of Technology , Hefei 230009 , China )
Abstract : Talbot -Lau X -ray imaging technology exhibits unique advantages in biomedical imaging by enabling simulta ⁃
neous acquisition of transmission , differential phase , and dark -field signals . However , phase stepping errors and X -ray 
source flux fluctuations induce moiré artifacts in reconstructed images , degrading image quality . To address this issue , an ar ⁃
tifact removal algorithm based on the correlation between the phase of the stepping curve and image artifacts is adopted , and 
its effectiveness is verified through simulation experiments . Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed algorithm can 
effectively eliminate artifacts caused by stepping errors and flux fluctuations while preserving the true information in images . 
Furthermore , by analyzing noise levels , fitting regions , and moiré fringe patterns , the algorithm's stability under different ex ⁃
perimental conditions is validated , highlighting its high practical application value .
Keywords : X-ray optics ；Talbot –Lau interferometer ；stepping errors ；flux fluctuations ；Moiré artifacts

1　X 射线光栅相位衬度成像
X 射线光栅相位衬度成像技术基于光栅干涉原
理，结合相位步进法对探测器捕获的光强数据进行
处理分析 ，从而实现样品多模态衬度图像的重构 。
